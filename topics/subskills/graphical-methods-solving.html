Solving equations graphically

Use the intersections of graphs to solve equations — including choosing which line to draw.

Solving equations graphically — the key ideas

Click each step below to reveal it — work through them in order the first time round.

Step 1 · Solutions are intersections

1 of 4▾

To solve $f(x) = g(x)$, draw $y = f(x)$ and $y = g(x)$. The $x$-coordinates of the points where they cross are the solutions. For $f(x) = 0$, the solutions are where the curve crosses the $x$-axis (the roots).

Step 2 · Choosing the line to draw

2 of 4▾

Given the graph of $y = x^2 - 2x - 3$, to solve $x^2 - 2x - 3 = 2$ you draw the horizontal line $y = 2$. To solve a different equation, rearrange it so one side matches the curve, e.g. $x^2 - 2x - 8 = 0$ becomes $x^2 - 2x - 3 = 5$, so draw $y = 5$.

Step 3 · How many solutions?

3 of 4▾

Count the intersections: a horizontal line can cut a $\cup$-shaped parabola twice (above the minimum), once (at the minimum) or not at all (below it). A line can cut a cubic up to three times.

Step 4 · Accuracy

4 of 4▾

Answers read from a graph are approximate — give them to a sensible accuracy (usually 1 or 2 decimal places) and say 'from the graph'. A larger, more careful graph gives better accuracy.

Worked example

Use the graph of $y = x^2 - 2x - 3$ to solve $x^2 - 2x - 3 = 2$.

Draw the line $y = 2$. It crosses the curve at $x \approx -1.45$ and $x \approx 3.45$.

So the solutions are $x \approx -1.45$ and $x \approx 3.45$. (Exactly, $x = 1 \pm \sqrt{6}$.)

Test yourself

Past-paper style question

The graph of $y = x^2 - 4x + 1$ is drawn.

(a) Use the graph to estimate the solutions of $x^2 - 4x + 1 = 0$.
(b) By drawing a suitable straight line, solve $x^2 - 4x - 2 = 0$. [5 marks]

Show the answer

▾

(a) Where the curve crosses the $x$-axis: $x \approx$ 0.27 and 3.73. (2 marks)

(b) Rearrange: $x^2 - 4x + 1 = 3$. Draw the line $y = 3$; it meets the curve at $x \approx$ −0.45 and 4.45. (3 marks: rearranging, line, solutions)

Practice

Solving equations graphically worksheet

Five short questions. Work through them, then reveal the mark scheme to check.

  1. To solve $x^2 - 3x + 2 = 4$ using the graph of $y = x^2 - 3x + 2$, which line should be drawn?
  2. The graphs of $y = f(x)$ and $y = g(x)$ intersect where $x = 1$ and $x = 4$. What are the solutions of $f(x) = g(x)$?
  3. The lines $y = 2x + 1$ and $y = x + 4$ intersect at (3, 7). Which equation does this solve, and what is the solution?
  4. A parabola has its minimum at (1, −4). How many solutions has $f(x) = -5$?
  5. Why are answers read from a graph only approximate?

Mark scheme

▾
  1. The horizontal line $y = 4$.
  2. $x = 1$ and $x = 4$.
  3. It solves $2x + 1 = x + 4$; the solution is $x = 3$.
  4. The line $y = -5$ is below the minimum, so there are no solutions.
  5. You can only read the position of the crossing point to the accuracy of the scale and your pencil, so the values are estimates.

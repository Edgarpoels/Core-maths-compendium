The inverse function ln and solving $a^x = b$

Use ln to undo $e^x$ and to solve equations where the unknown is in the power.

The inverse function ln and solving $a^x = b$ — the key ideas

Click each step below to reveal it — work through them in order the first time round.

Step 1 · ln undoes $e^x$

1 of 4▾

$\ln x$ (the natural logarithm) is the inverse function of $e^x$: $\ln(e^x) = x$ and $e^{\ln x} = x$. The graph of $y = \ln x$ is the reflection of $y = e^x$ in the line $y = x$. It is only defined for $x > 0$ and passes through $(1, 0)$.

Step 2 · Solving $e^{kt} = b$

2 of 4▾

To solve an equation like $e^{0.05t} = 3$, take $\ln$ of both sides: $0.05t = \ln 3$, so $t = \dfrac{\ln 3}{0.05} \approx 21.97$.

Step 3 · Solving $a^x = b$

3 of 4▾

For any base $a$, take logarithms of both sides: $x \ln a = \ln b$, so $x = \dfrac{\ln b}{\ln a}$. For example $2^x = 50$ gives $x = \dfrac{\ln 50}{\ln 2} \approx 5.64$.

Step 4 · Doubling and halving times

4 of 4▾

To find how long it takes to double at a growth multiplier $b$, solve $b^t = 2$: $t = \dfrac{\ln 2}{\ln b}$. The same method with $b^t = 0.5$ gives a half-life.

Worked example

Solve $2^x = 50$, giving your answer to 2 decimal places.

Take logs: $x \ln 2 = \ln 50$, so $x = \dfrac{\ln 50}{\ln 2} = \dfrac{3.9120}{0.6931} = $ 5.64.

Test yourself

Past-paper style question

£1000 is invested at 4% compound interest per year.

(a) Write down a formula for the value $V$ after $t$ years.
(b) Find how long it takes for the investment to reach £2000. Give your answer to 1 decimal place.
(c) Find the value of the investment after 18 years. [5 marks]

Show the answer

▾

(a) $V = 1000 \times 1.04^t$. (1 mark)

(b) $1.04^t = 2 \Rightarrow t = \dfrac{\ln 2}{\ln 1.04} = \dfrac{0.6931}{0.03922} = $ 17.7 years. (2 marks)

(c) $V = 1000 \times 1.04^{18} = $ £2025.82. (2 marks)

The inverse function ln and solving $a^x = b$ worksheet

Five short questions. Work through them, then reveal the mark scheme to check.

  1. Write down the value of $\ln(e^5)$.
  2. Solve $e^x = 10$, giving $x$ to 2 decimal places.
  3. Solve $3^x = 81$.
  4. Solve $5^x = 20$, giving $x$ to 2 decimal places.
  5. How long does money take to double at 7% compound interest per year? Give your answer to 2 decimal places.

Mark scheme

▾
  1. 5.
  2. $x = \ln 10 = $ 2.30.
  3. $3^4 = 81$, so $x = $ 4 (or $\ln 81 \div \ln 3$).
  4. $x = \dfrac{\ln 20}{\ln 5} = \dfrac{2.9957}{1.6094} = $ 1.86.
  5. $1.07^t = 2 \Rightarrow t = \dfrac{\ln 2}{\ln 1.07} = \dfrac{0.6931}{0.06766} = $ 10.24 years.

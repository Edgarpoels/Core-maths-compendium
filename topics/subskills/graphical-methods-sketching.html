Graph sketching and quadratic and cubic models — the key ideas

Click each step below to reveal it — work through them in order the first time round.

Step 1 · Key features to find

1 of 4▾

For a sketch, find: where the curve crosses the $y$-axis (put $x = 0$), where it crosses the $x$-axis (the roots: put $y = 0$), the turning points, and what happens at the ends. A sketch needs the correct shape and labelled key points, not exact accuracy.

Step 2 · Quadratic graphs

2 of 4▾

A quadratic $y = ax^2 + bx + c$ is a parabola: $\cup$ if $a > 0$ (a minimum) and $\cap$ if $a < 0$ (a maximum). If it factorises, $y = (x - p)(x - q)$ has roots $p$ and $q$. The turning point lies halfway between the roots; the $y$-intercept is $c$.

Step 3 · Cubic graphs

3 of 4▾

A cubic usually has an S-shape, going from bottom-left to top-right if the $x^3$ term is positive (the reverse if negative). It can cross the $x$-axis up to three times and has up to two turning points.

Step 4 · Quadratic models in context

4 of 4▾

Quadratics model the height of a thrown object, the area of a rectangle with a fixed perimeter, or profit against price. The turning point gives the greatest or least value; the roots show when the quantity is zero (for a ball: when it is launched and when it lands). Solve inequalities by looking at where the curve is above or below a line.

Worked example

Sketch $y = x^2 - 2x - 3$, marking the roots, the $y$-intercept and the turning point.

Factorise: $y = (x - 3)(x + 1)$, so the roots are $x = 3$ and $x = -1$.

$y$-intercept: $x = 0 \Rightarrow y = -3$.

Turning point: halfway between the roots at $x = 1$, where $y = 1 - 2 - 3 = -4$. The curve is a $\cup$ shape with minimum $(1, -4)$.

Test yourself

Past-paper style question

The height $h$ metres of a ball $t$ seconds after it is thrown is $h = 20t - 5t^2$.

(a) Sketch the graph for $0 \le t \le 4$, showing the greatest height and when the ball lands.
(b) For how long is the ball higher than 15 m? [5 marks]

Show the answer

▾

(a) $h = 5t(4 - t)$: roots $t = 0$ and $t = 4$, so the ball lands after 4 seconds. The turning point is at $t = 2$: $h = 40 - 20 = $ 20 m. ∩-shaped curve through (0, 0), (2, 20), (4, 0). (3 marks)

(b) $20t - 5t^2 = 15 \Rightarrow t^2 - 4t + 3 = 0 \Rightarrow (t - 1)(t - 3) = 0$, so $t = 1$ and $t = 3$. The ball is above 15 m between these, for 2 seconds. (2 marks)

Practice

Graph sketching and quadratic and cubic models worksheet

Five short questions. Work through them, then reveal the mark scheme to check.

  1. Find the roots of $y = (x - 2)(x + 5)$.
  2. Find the $y$-intercept of $y = x^2 + 3x - 4$.
  3. Find the turning point of $y = x^2 - 6x + 5$.
  4. Describe the shape of the graph of $y = x^3$.
  5. A ball's height is $h = 20t - 5t^2$. Find its height after 1 second.

Mark scheme

▾
  1. $x = 2$ and $x = -5$.
  2. Put $x = 0$: $y = $ −4.
  3. $y = (x - 1)(x - 5)$, roots 1 and 5. Turning point at $x = 3$: $y = 9 - 18 + 5 = -4$, so (3, −4) (a minimum).
  4. An S-shape through the origin, rising from bottom-left to top-right.
  5. $h = 20 - 5 = $ 15 m.

Gradient and zooming in — the key ideas

Click each step below to reveal it — work through them in order the first time round.

Step 1 · Rate of change

1 of 4▾

The rate of change of $y$ with respect to $x$ is $\dfrac{\text{change in } y}{\text{change in } x}$. Its units are '(units of $y$) per (unit of $x$)': kilometres per hour, pounds per month, degrees per minute.

Step 2 · Straight lines

2 of 4▾

On a straight-line graph the rate of change is constant: choose any two points and calculate rise ÷ run. A steeper line means a faster rate; a negative gradient means the quantity is falling.

Step 3 · Average rate on a curve (a chord)

3 of 4▾

On a curve the rate keeps changing. The average rate of change between two points is the gradient of the straight line joining them — a chord: $\dfrac{y_2 - y_1}{x_2 - x_1}$.

Step 4 · Zooming in

4 of 4▾

If you zoom in close to a point on a smooth curve it looks like a straight line. Taking a shorter and shorter chord gives gradients that settle towards one value — the gradient at that point, which is the gradient of the tangent.

Worked example

For $y = x^2$, find the average rate of change between $x = 1$ and $x = 3$, then between $x = 1$ and $x = 1.1$.

$x = 1 \to y = 1$; $x = 3 \to y = 9$: gradient $= \dfrac{9 - 1}{3 - 1} = 4$.

$x = 1.1 \to y = 1.21$: gradient $= \dfrac{1.21 - 1}{0.1} = 2.1$.

The shorter chord gives a value closer to the gradient at $x = 1$, which is 2.

Test yourself

Past-paper style question

The temperature at 9 am is 12 °C, at 11 am it is 15 °C and at 1 pm it is 24 °C.

(a) Find the average rate of change of temperature between 9 am and 1 pm, in °C per hour.
(b) Find the average rate between 9 am and 11 am, and between 11 am and 1 pm.
(c) When was the temperature rising faster? [5 marks]

Show the answer

▾

(a) $\dfrac{24 - 12}{4} = $ 3 °C per hour. (2 marks)

(b) 9–11 am: $\dfrac{15 - 12}{2} = $ 1.5 °C/h. 11 am–1 pm: $\dfrac{24 - 15}{2} = $ 4.5 °C/h. (2 marks)

(c) Between 11 am and 1 pm (4.5 > 1.5). (1 mark)

Practice

Gradient and zooming in worksheet

Five short questions. Work through them, then reveal the mark scheme to check.

  1. Find the gradient of the line through (2, 10) and (6, 26).
  2. A car travels 120 km in 2 hours at a constant speed. Write its rate of change of distance.
  3. For $y = x^2$, find the gradient of the chord from $x = 2$ to $x = 2.1$.
  4. For $y = x^2$, find the gradient of the chord from $x = 3$ to $x = 3.01$ and estimate the gradient at $x = 3$.
  5. A graph shows the amount in a savings account (£) against time (months). State the units of its gradient.

Mark scheme

▾
  1. $\dfrac{26 - 10}{6 - 2} = \dfrac{16}{4} = $ 4.
  2. $120 \div 2 = $ 60 km per hour.
  3. $\dfrac{4.41 - 4}{0.1} = $ 4.1.
  4. $\dfrac{9.0601 - 9}{0.01} = 6.01$, so the gradient at $x = 3$ is approximately 6.
  5. £ per month.

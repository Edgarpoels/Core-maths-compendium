Speed and acceleration

Use distance–time and speed–time graphs: gradient for speed and acceleration, area for distance.

Speed and acceleration — the key ideas

Click each step below to reveal it — work through them in order the first time round.

Step 1 · Distance–time graphs

1 of 4▾

On a distance–time graph the gradient is the speed. A straight line means constant speed, a flat line means stopped, and a steeper line means faster. Units: metres per second (m/s), kilometres per hour (km/h).

Step 2 · Speed–time graphs

2 of 4▾

On a speed–time graph the gradient is the acceleration (units m/s²). A positive gradient means speeding up, a flat line means constant speed, and a negative gradient means slowing down (deceleration).

Step 3 · Area under a speed–time graph

3 of 4▾

The area under a speed–time graph is the distance travelled. Split the area into rectangles, triangles and trapeziums: a rectangle is speed × time; a triangle is ½ × base × height.

Step 4 · Curved graphs

4 of 4▾

When a graph is curved the rate is not constant. The speed (or acceleration) at an instant is the gradient of the tangent at that point. The area under a curve can be estimated by counting squares or using trapeziums.

Worked example

A vehicle speeds up from 0 to 20 m/s in 4 s, travels at 20 m/s for 4 s, then slows to a stop in 4 s. Find the acceleration at the start and the total distance.

Acceleration $= \dfrac{20 - 0}{4} = $ 5 m/s².

Distance = area under graph = $\frac{1}{2} \times 4 \times 20 + 4 \times 20 + \frac{1}{2} \times 4 \times 20 = 40 + 80 + 40 = $ 160 m.

Test yourself

Past-paper style question

A car accelerates uniformly from rest to 12 m/s in 6 seconds, then travels at 12 m/s for 10 seconds.

(a) Find the acceleration in the first 6 seconds.
(b) Find the distance travelled in the first 6 seconds.
(c) Find the total distance travelled in the 16 seconds. [5 marks]

Show the answer

▾

(a) $\dfrac{12 - 0}{6} = $ 2 m/s². (1 mark)

(b) Area of triangle = $\frac{1}{2} \times 6 \times 12 = $ 36 m. (2 marks)

(c) Second stage: $12 \times 10 = 120$ m. Total = 36 + 120 = 156 m. (2 marks)

Practice

Speed and acceleration worksheet

Five short questions. Work through them, then reveal the mark scheme to check.

  1. A distance–time graph shows 50 m in 10 s as a straight line from the origin. Find the speed.
  2. A speed–time graph rises from 3 m/s to 15 m/s in 4 s. Find the acceleration.
  3. A vehicle travels at a constant 8 m/s for 5 s. Find the distance.
  4. A speed–time graph falls from 20 m/s to 0 in 5 s. Find the acceleration.
  5. A speed rises uniformly from 0 to 10 m/s in 5 s. Find the distance travelled in this time.

Mark scheme

▾
  1. $50 \div 10 = $ 5 m/s.
  2. $\dfrac{15 - 3}{4} = $ 3 m/s².
  3. $8 \times 5 = $ 40 m.
  4. $\dfrac{0 - 20}{5} = $ −4 m/s² (deceleration of 4 m/s²).
  5. $\frac{1}{2} \times 5 \times 10 = $ 25 m.
